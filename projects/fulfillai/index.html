Synthetic fulfillment data

I generated a deterministic fulfillment dataset with 50,000 synthetic orders. PostgreSQL and dbt organize the relational data and transformations. Redpanda and PySpark explore streaming, while MLflow and FastAPI support experiment tracking and serving.

The data is synthetic by design. It lets me test contracts and workflows without presenting the results as real customer outcomes.

Lower WAPE, higher RMSE

On 63,501 synthetic test rows, the hard-gated two-stage hurdle forecast lowered WAPE from 88.24% to 69.59% versus a rolling-28 baseline. Decisions and thresholds were frozen before the final chronological test.

What Actions and Argo CD each handle

Terraform provisions the GCP infrastructure. GitHub Actions validates the source and infrastructure, builds and scans the container, authenticates through OIDC / Workload Identity Federation, and promotes an exact image digest to Git-backed desired state.

Argo CD reconciles the Helm workload on GKE Autopilot. Actions owns build and promotion; Argo CD owns deployment. That distinction became useful when troubleshooting field ownership and rollout verification.

Recovering from a bad image

I introduced a nonexistent image and inspected ErrImagePull and ImagePullBackOff while healthy replicas continued serving. I restored a known-good release, checked recovery and restored Argo CD self-healing after the exercise.

Prometheus, Grafana and Alertmanager cover the observability path. A synthetic alert was routed end to end to Slack. The 99.9% SLO is a lab target used by alert rules, rather than a measured production uptime result.

What I verified in GKE

In GKE I verified API delivery, health, metrics and recovery. I still need to verify prediction with a loaded model artifact. The PostgreSQL, dbt and streaming checks ran locally.

I exercised HPA, PDB and RBAC in a local kind lab and checked the NetworkPolicy objects and selectors. A traffic test would be needed to verify enforcement. The complete V2 implementation remains on platform-engineering-v2.
